Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

In short
  • We store only what the diary needs: your profile and your food, water, weight, pill and workout entries.
  • Food photos are not stored: they go to Google's Gemini AI only for recognition.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or use third-party visitor counters.
  • You can delete all your data at any time — write to the author.

1. Who processes the data

The FITTER service is the Telegram bot @FitterFoodBot and its diary (a Telegram Mini App). The data operator is the project's author, Vladislav. For any data questions, contact: t.me/arkhitkovv.

The service's source code is open: github.com/sailxx/FITTER-AI. Everything described below can be verified in the code.

3. Photos and messages

A food photo is downloaded from Telegram, sent to Google Gemini for recognition and immediately removed from the server's memory — we do not store it. Only the result goes into the diary: the list of products and their nutrition values.

Text messages about food, questions to the bot and “What to eat” requests are also passed to Gemini. For an accurate answer, your profile (sex, age, height, weight, goal) and daily goal are sent along with them.

In the Fitter Android app, a photo from the camera or gallery is downscaled on the phone and likewise sent to Gemini, then immediately deleted from the server. The “What to eat” tips and the weekly review in the app are the same as in the bot.

Do not send the bot photos of people, documents or other personal information — the bot is meant for food only.

4. Who receives data

  • Telegram — you talk to the bot through it. Telegram's privacy policy applies.
  • Google (Gemini API) — recognizes photos and text and writes the tips and the weekly review. Google processes requests under the Gemini API terms. On the free tier Google may use requests to improve its services, including human review.
  • Cloudflare — the servers and database where the bot runs and the diary is stored. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies.
  • Open Food Facts — an open product database. Only the barcode number is sent there, with no data about you.

The servers of these companies may be located outside your country. By using the bot you agree to such transfer. Data is not passed to anyone else, except where the law directly requires it.

5. How long data is kept

  • Profile, diary, weight, pills and workouts — while you use the bot or until you ask for deletion.
  • “What to eat” options — 6 hours; the AI weekly review cache — 12 hours.
  • Technical error logs — a short period set by Cloudflare. Logs are used only to find failures.

6. How data is protected

  • The bot accepts requests only from Telegram, with a secret key.
  • The diary verifies Telegram's signature, so nobody else's diary can be opened. The signature is valid for no more than a day.
  • The Fitter Android app signs in to the diary only after you confirm the sign-in with a button in the bot. It gets its own access key; the server stores not the key itself but its hash. The key stops working after you sign out of the app or delete your data with /delete. Up to 5 phones can be connected; on the sixth, the oldest sign-in is disconnected.
  • The app stores on the phone only its access key and a copy of today's summary, so that widgets open without the internet. It requests no permissions except internet access. After you sign out, all of this is erased.
  • While the app is open it holds a secure connection to the server so that entries from the bot appear instantly. Only your day's summary is sent over it, and the connection closes together with the app.
  • Access keys are kept in Cloudflare's protected settings, not in the code.

7. Your rights

  • View your data — in the diary and the bot commands.
  • Correct — edit entries in the diary, redo the questionnaire with /reset.
  • Delete individual entries — in the diary; all your data at once — with the /delete command in the bot, immediately and irreversibly. If something didn't work, write to the author on Telegram.
  • Turn off reminders — in the bot settings. To stop the bot from writing to you at all, block it in Telegram.

8. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts — those services see your IP address under their own rules. We set no cookies and use no visitor counters. The diary stores only notes about which hints have been shown in the Telegram browser.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 14. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Important

FITTER's calorie counts are approximate. The service is not medical and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or dietitian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with its effective date. We will announce significant changes in the bot.
