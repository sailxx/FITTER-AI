Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find an answer? Message the author.

How do I count calories from a photo?

Open the FITTER bot in Telegram and send a photo of your dish. The AI recognizes the foods, estimates the portion weight and counts calories, protein, fat and carbs. The entry appears in your diary right away, where you can check and correct it.

How accurate is calorie counting from a photo?

A photo-based estimate is approximate and depends on the dish and the portion size. For better accuracy, add a caption to the photo (“200 g rice”) or fix the weight and product with the button under the entry. For packaged foods, the bot takes the values straight from the label. FITTER is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or dietitian.

How much does FITTER cost?

FITTER is currently free and ad-free. Open the bot in Telegram and start your diary.

Do I need to install an app?

No. FITTER works right inside Telegram, and the diary opens as a Telegram Mini App. No sign-up needed.

How does FITTER calculate my goal?

On first launch the bot asks 6 questions: sex, age, height, weight, activity and goal. Your calorie and macro goal is calculated with the Mifflin–St Jeor formula and recalculated whenever you log a new weight.

Can I correct a product or its weight?

Yes. Tap the product under the entry and enter the right weight, name or both, for example “trout 200 g”. Calories and macros are recalculated.

What about packaged foods?

Photograph the label — the bot copies the nutrition values exactly from it. If the label isn't visible, the bot reads the barcode and finds the product in the Open Food Facts database. You can also send the barcode digits as text.

Are there reminders to drink water?

Yes. Turn them on with the /remind command and pick an interval: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours or 2 hours. There are no reminders at night, and they stop once you reach your goal.

Are my photos stored?

No. The photo is passed to the AI only for recognition and is not stored. The diary keeps your profile and your food, water, weight and pill entries. You can delete your data at any time.
